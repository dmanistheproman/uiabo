# Sources to start reviewing

These pages were located on 29 September 2026. They are research starting points, not pre-filled answers. Check the exact wording, scope and effective date yourself. A source about a related topic may not settle the claim.

- Scam protection: [ScamShield](https://www.scamshield.gov.sg/) and [GovTech's description of ScamShield](https://www.tech.gov.sg/products-and-services/for-citizens/scam-prevention/scamshield/).
- Water supply: [Ministry of Sustainability and the Environment: Water](https://www.mse.gov.sg/policies/water/).
- Botanic Gardens: [NParks: Singapore Botanic Gardens](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/SBG).
- Emergency ambulance services: [SCDF: Emergency Medical Services](https://www.scdf.gov.sg/home/about-scdf/emergency-medical-services). Prefer current guidance over older handbooks for phone-service changes.
- Container deposits: [NEA: Beverage Container Return Scheme](https://www.nea.gov.sg/our-services/waste-management/beverage-container-return-scheme). Check amendments and transition dates rather than relying only on the original announcement.
- GST: [IRAS: Charging GST](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/goods-services-tax-%28gst%29/charging-gst-%28output-tax%29) and [IRAS: Exporting of Goods](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/goods-services-tax-%28gst%29/charging-gst-%28output-tax%29/when-to-charge-0-gst-%28zero-rate%29/exporting-of-goods).
- Government impersonation: [SPF advisory dated 4 May 2026](https://www.police.gov.sg/Media-Hub/News/2026/05/20260504_police_advisory_on_government_officials_impersonation_scams_involving_cash_transfers).
- SMS sender identity: [gov.sg SMS Sender ID](https://www.sms.gov.sg/).
- Path rules: [LTA: Rules and Code of Conduct](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/getting_around/active_mobility/rules_and_public_education/rules_and_code_of_conduct.html). Distinguish footpaths, pedestrian-only paths and cycling paths.
- Food safety: [SFA: Food Safety Tips](https://www.sfa.gov.sg/food-safety-tips/safe-food-practices/food-safety-tips) and [SFA: A–Z Food Safety](https://www.sfa.gov.sg/tools-and-resources/glossary-a-z-on-food-safety).
- Forecast regression: [NEA/MSS four-day forecast dataset](https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_f131f6e343bf8168e4057a04c4326a0a/view). Use the saved JSON snapshot rather than a webpage that changes during the review.

For remaining claims, locate authoritative evidence yourself. No source links or reviewer labels are passed to either pipeline by the comparison runner.
